# Modules

Every Halcyon coin pays 1% on every trade, to a pool Halcyon holds for it. The gardener collects it as ETH into `HalcyonFees`. 20% of every collect is the platform's. The other 80% does one of eight things, chosen at launch and changeable afterwards by the creator from the coin's page. A module is a number in `HalcyonFees.coins[token].module`; switching it moves nothing that already waits in the pot, it only changes what the gardener does with the pot from then on.

The names are the garden's, the one Ethereum calls itself (the infinite garden): the gardener tends every coin, and each module is one thing a garden does with what grows.

## 0 · Roots

For creators who fund their work with the coin: the 80% goes back to the roots. Every collect lands in HalcyonFees and 80% of it is pushed to the creator in the same transaction, with a 50,000 gas stipend. A creator that cannot receive ETH that way (a contract that refuses it, a wallet whose `receive` is expensive) gets a claimable balance instead, withdrawn with `claim()` from the site's Me page. The creator can hand the coin to another address with `setCreator`.

## 1 · Rain

For coins that want to pay their community for holding: the 80% rains on every holder. It waits in the coin's pot; when the pot is worth paying (`HALCYON_PAYOUT_MIN_ETH`, 0.05 ETH by default), the gardener reads every holder from its index, computes each share pro rata to the balance, drops the shares below `HALCYON_PAYOUT_MIN_SHARE_ETH` (they stay in the pot for the next round, so dust wallets neither cost gas nor lose their part) and pays the rest in ETH in batches of `HALCYON_PAYOUT_BATCH` (`payHolders`). The contract checks every recipient holds the coin at the moment of payment and that a batch never exceeds the pot. Holders that are contracts which refuse ETH get a claimable balance.

The pool, the dead address, the launchpad, the fees contract, the lockers, the routers and the coin itself are never holders.

## 2 · Prune

For coins that want a shrinking supply. The 80% waits in the pot; past the threshold the gardener buys the coin back through its own pool (SwapRouter02 on v3, HalcyonSwap on v4, with a minimum out from the pool math) and burns what it bought (`buyback`). Every prune is an event (`Burned(token, eth, coins)`) and the coin page lists them.

## 3 · Harvest

For coins that want to pay their holders in something other than themselves: a tokenized stock, an ERC-20 on Ethereum issued against real shares. The platform allows stocks one by one on chain (`setStockAllowed`) and keeps a route for each in the registry (`scripts/stocks.mjs`, seeded from `eth/stocks-seed.json`): a Uniswap v3 path from WETH, or Uniswap v4 hops from ETH or WETH, one per pool, each with the pool's fee, tick spacing and hooks; a creator picks one of the allowed, routed stocks at launch or when switching.

The 80% waits in the pot. Past the threshold the gardener buys the stock along the registered route, at most `HALCYON_STOCK_MAX_BUY_ETH` at a time (the routes are thin), with a minimum out from the matching quoter: through Uniswap v3's SwapRouter02 along a path (`buyStock`, quoted by QuoterV2), or through Uniswap v4 along hops (`buyStockV4`, quoted by the v4 quoter), which HalcyonSwap walks inside one PoolManager lock (`swapPath`: each hop's pool from the currency before it, the input settled once, the output taken once; what the first pool cannot take goes back into the pot, a later pool that cannot take its whole input makes the buy revert rather than strand anything). Either way the stock is held by HalcyonFees for that coin (`stockHeld`). Then it is paid out pro rata to holders with at least 10,000 coins (0.001% of the supply), checked on chain to hold the coin at payment (`payStock`). A holder the issuer will not let receive (a compliance block, a paused token) gets a claimable balance (`claimStock`) instead of blocking the batch. Both steps are events (`StockBought`, `StockPaid`).

What is actually on Ethereum mainnet (checked October 2026): Ondo Stocks tokens (450 of them on mainnet, freely transferable outside the US, every one of them in the seed registry with the issuer's logo, from the issuer's official token list v13.0.0) are the only tokenized stocks with Uniswap liquidity, and only a few have real depth on v3: SPCXon (about $350K), NVDAon ($280K), SLVon ($535K, a silver ETF), TSLAon and SPYon (thin). Those five carry a route in the seed; the other 445 (333 stocks, 103 ETFs, 14 commodity funds) wait for a pool. The stocks themselves need no pool to exist (an issued token is an ERC-20 like any other, and another launchpad can pair a new coin against AAPLon with no AAPLon market anywhere); what needs a pool is *buying* one for holders with ETH, and Ondo's own primary market (signed issuer quotes, for authorized callers, USDC to USDon to the stock) is not a route a contract can take. So a pool on Uniswap v3 or v4 is what counts, and the chain itself says where they are. Three script commands keep the list alive:

- `node scripts/stocks.mjs import --ondo` refreshes the registry from the issuer's list on GitHub (or `--file` for a saved token list or catalog CSV), keeping every route already recorded.
- `node scripts/stocks.mjs catch --all` asks the chain, stock by stock: the v3 factory for pools against WETH, USDC and USDT, and the v4 PoolManager's own `Initialize` log for every pool ever opened with the stock on either side (the stock is an indexed topic, so an RPC that indexes logs answers each in one call; one that limits the range gets it split). Every pool with liquidity in range gets a route from ETH (v4: the pool's other side is ETH or WETH, or has a plain ETH pool at a usual tier, or is reached through USDC; v3: WETH, USDC, USDT, or anything with a USDC pool) and a quote for `--probe` ETH (0.05 by default) through the matching quoter; the pool that gives the most stock wins if the probe moves its price at most `--max-impact` percent (3 by default), and `--go` records it with a note and prints the allow calldata. A hooked v4 pool is used as it is (its hook may refuse the swap, which the quote shows).
- `node scripts/stocks.mjs discover --all --min-usd 50000` does the same from DexScreener's pair list instead of the chain's log: the deepest Uniswap pool, v3 or v4, over the floor that a route from ETH can reach, confirmed on chain (the v3 factory; a v4 pool's key from the usual tiers or from its `Initialize` log), quoted, and with `--go` recorded.

A stock with no pool on any DEX, or pools with nothing in them, or pools only another DEX has, is reported as such. xStocks are on Ethereum but rebase and have no AMM depth; Backed's bTokens are being wound down; Dinari and Superstate tokens need an allowlist. Tokenized stocks are issued by third parties under their own terms; Ondo bars US persons, so paying its tokens to holders is a matter for the platform's legal review before any stock is allowed on chain.

## 4 · Branch

For teams, charities, a treasury and a founder: the 80% branches. The creator names up to eight addresses and their shares in basis points, adding up to 10,000. Every collect is pushed to them at once in those shares, with the same stipend and claimable fallback as Roots. The creator can change the branches (`setBranches`); a coin launched with the Branch module must carry them in its launch parameters.

## 5 · Clover

For coins that want a reason to hold and a story to tell: one lucky holder a round. The 80% waits in the pot. When it is worth paying, the gardener opens a draw (`openDraw`): the contract records the block three ahead as the draw block. Once that block is in (and for 250 blocks after, while its hash is available), the gardener pays the whole pot to the holder the hash drew (`payDraw`): holders sorted by address, each weighted by balance, the hash modulo the total balance picks a point on that line, and the holder it falls in wins. The contract checks the winner holds the coin and records the block, the hash and the winner in the event (`DrawPaid`), so anyone can recompute the draw from the holders at that block. A draw left unpaid past its window simply expires and a new one opens.

## 6 · Rings

For coins that want to reward the patient, like a tree's rings. Paid through the same `payHolders` as Rain, but the gardener weighs each holder by balance times days held: a day at least, thirty at most. A wallet that has held for a month weighs thirty times one that bought today, for the same bag. The indexer dates a holding from the transfer that took the balance from zero; selling to zero resets it.

## 7 · Mist

For coins whose holders would rather not have their income read off the chain. Mist is real privacy of the kind Privacy Pools and Zcash have, in Halcyon's own shape: payouts are born as private notes in a pool and leave it by zero-knowledge proof, so nobody, the gardener included, can tell which withdrawal was whose. It hides no trade and no balance; what it hides is who was paid what.

**The key.** A holder registers a mist key once with `HalcyonFees.setMistKey(bytes)`: 64 bytes, a spending public key and a viewing public key, both points of Baby Jubjub (the curve the circuit speaks), packed the way circomlib packs them. The site derives the two private keys in the browser from one wallet signature of a fixed sentence (`shared/mist.mjs` `SIGN_MESSAGE`), so the same wallet always derives the same keys on any device and nothing has to be stored; an empty key removes it. The contract checks the shape; a key that is not on the curve simply never gets a note, and its holder is paid in the open.

**The pool.** `HalcyonMist` is one contract for the whole launchpad: a Merkle tree of notes, Poseidon over BN254, 22 levels (a tree of 65,536 batches of 64 notes), the last 64 roots remembered so a proof built a few batches ago still goes through, the spent nullifiers, and a fixed set of denominations in ETH chosen at deploy (`HALCYON_MIST_DENOMS`, 0.01, 0.1 and 1 by default). Only HalcyonFees can sow into it, and nothing but a proof can take out of it: no owner, no upgrade, no pause.

**The round.** When a Mist coin's pot is worth paying, the gardener computes every holder's exact share. A holder with a valid key gets theirs as notes: the share broken into the pool's denominations, largest first, and the remainder as one more smallest note with the probability of the remainder over the smallest denomination (so over the rounds everyone gets exactly their due and nothing has to be remembered; the draw comes from the latest block hash and the address), never more than the pot, the rest waiting for the next round; at most `HALCYON_MIST_MAX_NOTES` (64) notes a round, the largest first when there are more. For every note the gardener draws a fresh ephemeral key, computes a secret it shares with the holder's viewing key (Diffie-Hellman on Baby Jubjub, hashed), and the note's commitment, `Poseidon(Poseidon(spending public key), secret)`. It shuffles the notes and sends `payMist(token, commits, denoms, ephemerals, viewTags)`; HalcyonFees takes the total from the pot and calls `HalcyonMist.sow` with it. The pool checks every denomination and the value, hashes every note into a leaf (`Poseidon(commitment, denomination)`), the batch into a subtree and the subtree into the tree, and announces each note (`Note(batch, index, token, commit, denom, ephemeral, viewTag)`) so any browser can rebuild the tree. Holders without a key are paid their exact share through `payHolders`, in the open, in the same tick.

**The holder's side.** The site's Me page asks for the signature, derives the keys and keeps them in memory for the tab. It reads the public feed of notes (`/api/mist/notes`, the `Note` events, in tree order) and tries each one with the viewing key: one multiplication and two hashes per note, one byte compared with the view tag, the commitment recomputed on a match. A note that is theirs is spent with a proof the browser makes (snarkjs, Groth16, the circuit's wasm and zkey served from `/zk/`, about two seconds): it rebuilds the tree from the feed and proves that some leaf of a root the pool knows is a note for its spending key, revealing only a nullifier (`Poseidon(spending key, secret, leaf index)`, which only the spending key can make, so a note spends once) and the public inputs the proof binds: the root, the denomination, the destination address, the relayer and the relayer's fee. The destination is any address or ENS name. The site's relayer (`/api/mist/quote`, `/api/mist/relay`) verifies the proof and sends `HalcyonMist.withdraw` with the gardener's key, paying the gas for the fee the proof fixed (its gas plus `HALCYON_MIST_RELAY_MARGIN_PCT`, never more than half the note); or the holder sends the proof from a wallet of their own and pays the gas. The pool verifies the proof on chain (`MistVerifier`, generated by snarkjs from the circuit), refuses a spent nullifier, a stale root, a wrong denomination or a fee above the note, pays the destination the denomination minus the fee and the relayer the fee, and emits `Spent`.

**What is public, what is not.** Public: every note's commitment, denomination, batch and coin; every withdrawal's denomination, destination, relayer and fee; who holds a mist key (the key is on chain). Not public: which note is whose, and which withdrawal spent which note. The crowd a withdrawal hides in is every unspent note of that denomination ever sown, across every Mist coin, which is why the pool is one for the whole launchpad and the denominations are few.

**What to know.** The gardener computes the notes, so the platform's gardener knows who it sowed for, in memory, at that moment; it keeps no record of it, its ledger names counts and denominations, not holders, and its log never prints a note's holder. The chain cannot check a note's owner (that is the point), so Mist is the one module where the gardener's recipients are not verified by the contract: a dishonest gardener could sow a round to keys of its own, and what every holder can verify is that their own notes arrive and add up to their share. Small rounds are small crowds: a withdrawal right after a round, of a denomination only one holder got, tells a lot; wait, and spend when the crowd has grown. The relayer sees the IP of whoever relays through it and nothing a block explorer would not; anyone may run one, and a funded fresh wallet needs none. The circuit (`zk/circuits/withdraw.circom`, 7,106 constraints) needs a trusted setup: `zk/build.mjs --dev` makes a powers of tau on one machine for tests and demos, and `zk/setup.json` records that; a deploy to mainnet must be built on a public powers of tau prepared for phase 2 (`node zk/build.mjs --ptau ppot_0080_14.ptau`, the Ethereum Foundation PSE's Perpetual Powers of Tau, or Hermez's `powersOfTau28_hez_final_14.ptau` where it still answers; the README says where; with a phase 2 contribution of its own, more with `snarkjs zkey contribute`), and `scripts/deploy.mjs` refuses a development setup on mainnet. The circuit and the contracts are unaudited.

**Check it yourself.** `node scripts/mist.mjs check` reads the pool on chain (verifier, Poseidon, denominations, batches, root) and says whether the verifier's code on chain is, byte for byte, the MistVerifier compiled in this folder from the recorded setup. `node scripts/mist.mjs prove` makes a note here, proves its withdrawal in the terminal (about two seconds), verifies the proof with the verification key, then asks the verifier on mainnet with an `eth_call`: true; and shows the pool's two further checks that would stop that withdrawal all the same (a root the pool never sowed, a nullifier). `node scripts/mist.mjs trail 0xTOKEN` lists a coin's trail with every transaction: the holders' `setMistKey`, the rounds sown (`payMist`, with batch, notes and root), the payments in the open, and the pool's spends. `node scripts/mist.mjs spends` lists the notes spent from the pool: each a proof the verifier accepted. The verifier's source is verified on Etherscan, so the same reading is possible with no code of ours at all.

## What the gardener cannot do

The gardener is one address the platform runs. The contract lets it move a coin's pot only into that coin's holders, that coin's buyback or that coin's stock, with every recipient checked to hold the coin, or, under Mist, into notes of the pool's denominations whose owners it cannot verify (see above). It cannot send a pot anywhere else, cannot touch the platform's share, cannot change a module, a branch or a creator, cannot touch the liquidity (nobody can), cannot touch a coin's supply beyond burning what it bought, and cannot take anything out of the mist pool without a proof. The platform wallet can rotate the gardener (`setGardener`), allow stocks and withdraw the platform pot, and nothing else.
