## 0.8.1

- **The gardener's patience.** The thresholds (`HALCYON_COLLECT_MIN_ETH` 0.02, `HALCYON_PAYOUT_MIN_ETH` 0.05) say when a coin is tended at once; under them a coin is now still tended every `HALCYON_GARDENER_PATIENCE_MIN` minutes (fifteen) as long as what is due is worth ten times its gas at the gas price of the moment (`HALCYON_PAYOUT_MAX_GAS_PCT`), so a small coin on Rain, Rings, Prune, Harvest, Clover or Mist sees its fees collected and its module paid at a steady rhythm instead of waiting for a pot it may never fill. ## 0.6.4

- **The gardener's transactions, one at a time, followed until they are in.** `eth/sender.mjs` sends every gardener transaction (and every relayed mist withdrawal) with fees of its own: twice the base fee plus a tip (the node's estimate, at least `HALCYON_GARDENER_TIP_GWEI`, 0.1 gwei), under `HALCYON_GAS_CAP_GWEI`, so the next rise in the base fee does not strand it; the nonce is read from the chain. A transaction not in a block after two minutes is kept on disk as pending (`DATA_DIR/halcyon-pending.json`), its ledger entry says so, and every tick follows it: in a block, the entry is settled; out for two and a half minutes since it was last sent, it is sent again at the same nonce with fees fifteen percent higher and at least the fees of now; its nonce taken by a transaction that is none of its own, it is given up and reported (the key was used elsewhere). Nothing else goes out while one is pending, so a stuck collect no longer piles a second collect of the same coin behind it every minute; a transaction waiting at the gardener's nonce that the process never sent (one from before this version) is replaced by the next one sent. `/api/gardener` shows `pending`; the tick logs `waiting for …`. `/api/mist/relay` answers with the hash after 45 seconds at most (`block` 0 while the withdrawal is still out) instead of holding the request for the receipt. On mainnet the gardener's collects were timing out after three minutes at the fees viem picks by default and being sent again each tick; this is the fix.
- 11 module checks (the sender on the in-process chain with mining off: the fees, a pending transaction bumped at the same nonce and settled in the ledger once mined, a nonce taken by another transaction given up and reported, a transaction waiting at the nonce replaced, the tick waiting).

## 0.5.0

Harvest through Uniswap v4, and the chain as the map.

- **`buyStockV4`.** A stock's route may now be Uniswap v4 hops from ETH or WETH (`v4: { from, hops: [{ currency, fee, tickSpacing, hooks }] }` in the registry) as well as a v3 path from WETH. `HalcyonFees.buyStockV4(token, eth, minOut, fromWeth, hops)` buys along them through `HalcyonSwap.swapPath`, a multi-hop exact-input swap inside one PoolManager lock (each hop's pool from the currency before it, the input settled once, the output taken once); a route must end at the coin's stock; what the first pool cannot take goes back into the pot, a later pool that cannot take its whole input makes the buy revert (`thin`) rather than strand anything; hooked pools are walked as they are. `swapPath` is public like `buy` and `sell`. ## 0.4.0

The garden, and real privacy.

- **The garden's names.** Nothing in Halcyon carries a word from anywhere else now. The keeper is the **gardener**. The eight modules are **Roots** (the creator keeps), **Rain** (every holder, pro rata), **Prune** (buy back and burn), **Harvest** (a tokenized stock for the holders), **Branch** (up to eight addresses), **Clover** (one holder a round, by a block hash), **Rings** (balance times time held) and **Mist**; the contract's constants, functions and events follow (`MODULE_ROOTS`…`MODULE_MIST`, `setBranches`, `openDraw`/`payDraw`, `DrawOpened`/`DrawPaid`), as do the API's module ids, the env names (`HALCYON_GARDENER_*`) and every page. Ethereum calls itself the infinite garden; so does this.
- **Mist, the private module, is a zero-knowledge pool now.** Veil's stealth coupons are gone; in their place `HalcyonMist`: one pool for the whole launchpad, a Poseidon Merkle tree of notes in fixed denominations (`HALCYON_MIST_DENOMS`, 0.01, 0.1 and 1 ETH by default), the last 64 roots, the spent nullifiers, a Groth16 verifier (`MistVerifier`, generated from `zk/circuits/withdraw.circom`, 7,106 constraints) and a Poseidon contract. A holder registers a mist key once (two Baby Jubjub public keys, derived in the browser from one signature). Each round the gardener breaks the keyed holders' shares into notes (largest first, the remainder by lot), makes a fresh commitment per note with a secret shared with the holder's viewing key, shuffles them and sows them through `payMist`; the pool hashes them into its tree and announces each with its ephemeral key and view tag. The holder's browser finds its notes with the viewing key, rebuilds the tree, and spends a note with a proof it makes itself (snarkjs; the wasm and zkey are served from `/zk/`): the proof reveals a nullifier and the destination, the relayer and the fee, nothing else, so no one can tell which withdrawal was whose, the gardener included. The site's relayer (`/api/mist/quote`, `/api/mist/relay`) verifies the proof and sends it with the gardener's key for a fee the proof fixed; or the holder sends it from a wallet of their own. Holders without a key are paid in the open. `docs/modules.md` says what is public, what is not, and what to trust.

## 0.3.0

- **Veil, the eighth module.** Holders are paid as coupons of one size, each to a one-time address only its holder can find (ERC-5564 stealth addresses, scheme 1 with view tags). A holder registers a veil key once (`HalcyonFees.setVeilKey`: two compressed public keys, derived in the browser from one wallet signature, never stored); each round the gardener computes exact shares, rounds the keyed holders' shares to coupons by lot (nothing is lost in expectation, nothing is remembered), derives a fresh address per coupon, shuffles them and pays through `payVeil`, announcing each coupon (`Veiled`, and the chain's ERC-5564 announcer when set); holders without a key get their exact share in the open. The chain cannot check a coupon's owner, which is the point; the docs say what that means. The site's Me page derives the keys from the signature, scans the public feed of coupons with the viewing key in the browser and sweeps each coupon with its own key to any address or ENS name through the site's relay, never through the wallet. `docs/modules.md` has the full model and its limits.

## 0.2.0

The model changed: no curve of its own, the pool is the curve.

- **Launch straight into Uniswap, locked.** A launch clones the coin, mints the supply and puts all of it into one single-sided position of a 1% pool at the market cap the creator chose in dollars (Chainlink at launch; presets $4K, $5K, $7K, $10K). The position is held by a locker with no way to move it. No token tax any more: the fee is the pool's 1%, all of it the coin's because Halcyon holds all the liquidity; the gardener collects it and the 80/20 applies.
- **Two pools.** Standard (Uniswap v3 against WETH) and Rules (Uniswap v4 against ETH with the Halcyon hook: an opening fee on buys that falls to 1% over a window the creator sets, a max per swap during it, a sell fee up to 5%, and one liquidity provider). The rules need no knowledge of the trader, so they hold for every router. A rules launch costs about 760k gas, a v3 launch about 5.6M.
- **Three new modules.** Split (up to eight addresses in the creator's shares, pushed on every collect), Jackpot (the pot to one holder drawn by a committed block's hash, weighted by balance, checkable by anyone) and Diamond (holders weighted by balance times days held, up to thirty).
- **Stocks.** The registry is seeded with what is actually on Ethereum mainnet: Ondo Stocks, five with Uniswap v3 routes (SPCXon, NVDAon, TSLAon, SPYon, SLVon) and twenty more waiting for one, with the issuer's logos. Stock payouts that an issuer blocks become claimable instead of failing the batch; stock buys are capped per transaction.
- **Contracts**: `HalcyonToken` (plain ERC-20), `HalcyonFees` (seven modules, `wire`, `claimStock`, jackpot draws, splits, `poolKey`), `Halcyon` (the launchpad: `launch` with the cap, the pool, the rules and the split; `predict` for the salt), `HalcyonLocker`, `HalcyonV4Locker`, `HalcyonHook`, `HalcyonSwap`, `Create2Deployer`. Compiled against `@uniswap/v4-core`; the standard input for verification inlines every imported source.
- **Server**: the indexer follows v3 pool swaps and the PoolManager's swaps per pool id, the lockers' collects, and since when each holder holds; the gardener collects through the lockers (simulating first for the minimum out), pays holders and diamond weights, burns through either pool, buys and pays stock, runs jackpot draws. `/api/quote` runs the pool math with the live opening fee and the max per swap.
- **Site**: the home page is the hero, the numbers and the coins; the launch wizard has the starting cap, the pool and its rules, seven modules with a stock picker (logos) and a split editor; the coin page shows the pool, the opening countdown, the rules, the trades with their fee, the module's record (splits, draws), the holders' holding time; filters by pool; halcyon.cash and @halcyoncash in the footer and the meta tags.
- **Scripts**: `deploy.mjs` deploys the seven in order, mines the hook's address and wires; `--no-v4`. `stocks.mjs` gained `seed`, `--logo`, `--issuer`, `--category`.
- **Tests**: 18 contract checks on the real v3 and v4 bytecode, 7 server checks, 10 deploy checks, 49 browser checks.
